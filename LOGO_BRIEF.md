# Profindle — Logo Design Brief

## The company
**Profindle** is a **Thai B2B marketplace** that connects businesses (buyers) with **verified service providers** — agencies, IT, accountants, law firms, event companies, and 400+ services. Buyers post one request that instantly reaches matching providers on **LINE**; providers get warm, verified leads. Tagline energy: **"Find the right business partner. Fast."**

## The one-line concept the logo must express
**Quick · Trusted · Collaboration** — *find the right partner, fast, and know they're verified.*

- **Quick** → motion, forward momentum, speed, decisiveness.
- **Trusted** → verified, credible, stable, professional (we verify every provider via DBD registration).
- **Collaboration** → two sides coming together, a match, partnership, a deal done.

## Style direction
- **Modern, clean, minimal, geometric** — think premium SaaS (Notion, Figma, Remote, Calendly, Genlo).
- Professional and **trustworthy** enough for enterprise buyers, approachable enough for a 2-person studio.
- A strong **icon/monogram/abstract mark** that pairs with a clean bold wordmark ("Profindle").
- The mark should feel **confident and simple**, not busy or literal.
- **Avoid a literal magnifying glass** (too on-the-nose for "find").

## Colors (use these exact values)
| Role | HEX | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| **Primary teal** | `#0F6F73` (gradient to `#1A9DA3` optional) | Main brand color, the mark |
| **Accent orange** | `#F77F00` | ONE confident accent only — never a background wash |
| **Ink / near-black** | `#171A21` | Wordmark on light backgrounds |

Keep it to **teal + one orange accent + ink**. Don't add extra colors.

## Must-haves (non-negotiable)
1. Works in **one color** (solid teal) and in **monochrome black/white**.
2. Legible and recognizable at **tiny sizes** — must survive as a **16×16 favicon** and a **square app icon / LINE OA avatar**.
3. A **standalone mark** (no text) that can be used alone as the app icon.
4. Looks good on **light AND dark** backgrounds (provide a white/reverse version).
5. Wordmark set in a clean **geometric or humanist sans** (e.g. Inter, Manrope, Poppins), letters **outlined to vector paths** (not live text).

## Deliverables
- Primary horizontal logo (mark + "Profindle" wordmark)
- Mark only (icon)
- Square app-icon version (rounded tile)
- Favicon
- White / reverse version for dark backgrounds
- Monochrome (1-color) version
- Vector source: **SVG** (and PNG exports)

## Do / Don't
- ✅ Do: one clear idea, generous negative space, geometric precision, teal + a single orange accent.
- ✅ Do: make the mark meaningful (motion / a match / a checkmark of trust / two shapes joining).
- ❌ Don't: literal magnifying glass, clip-art icons, gradients with 3+ colors, thin fragile lines, mascots (save the capybara for a separate mascot later), drop shadows, or anything that won't read at 16px.
