The identity system for Thailand's B2B marketplace.

FIND.CONNECT.GROW.
Two teal arms + an orange spark — an open, welcoming figure. Search made human: find the right partner, together.
01

Logo

The mark and the wordmark lock up as one unit. Lead with the full logo; use the mark alone only where space is tight (app icon, avatar, favicon).

Mark
Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the orange dot. Never place the logo on a busy photo without a solid or scrim panel behind it.

02

Color

Teal is the brand — trust, calm, professionalism. Orange is the single accent: use it for one thing per view (a CTA, the "find" moment, the spark). Ink and cool-grey neutrals do the quiet work.

03

Typography

One family, two roles. Manrope (or Inter) — modern, clean, professional, and fully legible in Thai and English. Weights carry the hierarchy; keep tracking tight on headlines.

04

App icon & favicon

The mark reversed to white on a teal tile. It stays recognizable down to 16px — use it for the LINE OA avatar, app icon, and browser favicon.

Tile corner radius ≈ 22%. On the tile the arms are white and the dot stays orange.

05

Tagline & voice

Tagline
FIND. CONNECT. GROW.

Find a verified partner → connect on LINE → grow your business. Keep the three words together; accent only "CONNECT" in orange.

Voice
  • Professional & trustworthy — real deals, real money.
  • Fast & clear — say what happens, plainly.
  • Bilingual by default — Thai and English, both intentional.

07

Do & Don't

✓ Do
  • Use the master SVG at any size — it's crisp everywhere.
  • Keep clear space around the logo.
  • Use the white/reverse version on dark or teal grounds.
  • Let orange be the single accent per view.
✕ Don't
  • Don't recolor the mark or swap the palette.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, or add shadows/outlines.
  • Don't place the colored mark on a low-contrast background.
  • Don't rebuild the wordmark in a different font.
