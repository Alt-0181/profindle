01What Profindle is (and isn't)

We're a directory + broadcast tool. We don't broker, contract, or take a cut.

Profindle is an online platform where Thai businesses can list their services, get found by potential clients, and broadcast project requests to a relevant audience. We connect people. We don't sit between you on the actual work.

What we do:

  • Host your company profile, services, and portfolio
  • Push your broadcast requests to matching providers via LINE
  • Offer search, filtering, and a Verified badge for credibility

What we don't do:

  • We don't sign contracts on your behalf, hold funds, or process payments between you and another company
  • We don't guarantee work, leads, conversions, or response rates
  • We don't audit or endorse the quality of any company's work — verification only confirms a real DBD-registered business exists

If you do business with someone you found here, that contract is strictly between you and them. We're not a party to it.

03Verification & the Verified badge

The badge means your DBD certificate checked out. It's not a quality stamp.

You can upload your DBD (กรมพัฒนาธุรกิจการค้า) certificate for review. Our team checks that the document is valid, current, and matches the company name on your profile. If it does, we grant a Verified badge.

The badge tells other users that a real, registered Thai business is behind this profile. It does not mean we've checked the quality of their work, the truth of their claims, or whether they'll deliver on a project. Use your own judgement before signing anything.

Heads up: uploading a forged, expired, or someone else's certificate is grounds for permanent ban and will be reported to the relevant authorities.

06Broadcasts & messaging

Broadcasts are short project briefs you send to providers in a chosen category. We deliver them via LINE — the same channel they use for normal Profindle alerts.

  • Free plan: 4 broadcasts per calendar month. Premium: unlimited.
  • Description has a 300-character limit. Be specific.
  • You can include a budget range or pick "To be discussed" — your call.
  • The recipient sees your name and company; we don't anonymise broadcasts.
  • Spamming the same brief, fake briefs, or briefs unrelated to the chosen category will get the broadcast feature disabled on your account.

Replies happen on LINE, off the platform. We can't see those conversations and we can't moderate them — handle each contact the way you would any other business outreach.
