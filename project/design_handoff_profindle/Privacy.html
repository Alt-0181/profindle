01What we collect

Account info you give us, content you upload, and basic usage logs.

02Why we collect it

Each piece of data has a specific job:

  • To run your account — let you log in, save your profile, send broadcasts
  • To match you with relevant companies — show your profile to people searching your category, push broadcasts to relevant providers
  • To verify your business — review your DBD certificate and grant the Verified badge
  • To improve the platform — analyse anonymised usage to fix bugs and decide what to build next
  • To prevent abuse — block spam, fake accounts, scraping
  • To meet legal obligations — keep tax invoices, respond to lawful requests

Under Thailand's PDPA, our legal bases are contract (we need this to provide the service you signed up for), legitimate interest (running and protecting the platform), consent (where we explicitly ask), and legal obligation (where Thai law requires it).

03Who sees it

Other Profindle users see your public profile. A few service providers see what they need to do their job. That's it.

Other Profindle users see what's on your public profile — company name, services, portfolio, contact info you've chosen to display, Verified badge. They do not see your email password, DBD certificate, billing details, or any data that's not on your profile.

Service providers we share data with:

  • LINE — to deliver broadcast notifications and let users connect their LINE account
  • Our hosting provider — stores the database and uploaded files
  • Our email provider — sends OTP codes, broadcast alerts, and product emails
  • Our analytics provider — aggregated, anonymised usage data only
  • Our payment processor (for paid plans) — handles your card; we never see the number

We have written agreements with each partner setting limits on how they can use the data. We will never sell or share your personal data, except where legally required (court orders, tax authorities) or to protect the safety of our users.

04Cookies & analytics

We use only the cookies we need:

  • Essential — keep you logged in, remember your language preference. Always on.
  • Analytics — help us see which pages people use. Anonymised. You can opt out from the cookie banner.

We don't use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

06How we protect it

We take security seriously:

  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes (we never see your actual password)
  • Uploaded documents (like DBD certificates) are stored encrypted
  • Access to production data is limited to a small number of staff and logged
  • We run regular backups and test them
If a breach happens that affects your data, we'll tell you within 72 hours of detecting it, as required by PDPA, and explain what we're doing about it.

07Your rights under PDPA

You can see, fix, export, or delete your data — anytime, free of charge.

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access — ask for a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correct — update anything that's wrong (most fields you can edit yourself in Settings)
  • Delete — close your account and have your data removed (with the exceptions noted above)
  • Export — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object — tell us to stop using your data for a specific purpose (e.g. analytics)
  • Withdraw consent — for anything we do based on consent, you can change your mind anytime
  • Complain — to the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) of Thailand if you think we've mishandled your data

Most of these you can do yourself in Settings. For anything else, email us at privacy@profindle.co — we'll respond within 30 days.

08International transfers

Your data is stored on servers in Singapore (our hosting provider's nearest region). Some of our service providers (e.g. our email sender) may process data in the United States or the European Union. In all cases we use providers with PDPA-compatible safeguards in place — standard contractual clauses or equivalent.

09Children

Profindle is for businesses and adult professionals. We do not collect data from anyone under 18.

10Changes to this policy

If we change anything material — what we collect, who we share it with, how long we keep it — we'll email you at least 14 days before the change takes effect. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
