## The Problem

Finding — and being found for — B2B services in Thailand is slow, noisy, and built on trust you can't verify.

- **Buyers waste days hunting.** Finding a reliable agency, studio, accountant, or law firm means digging through Google, Facebook groups, and word-of-mouth referrals — then vetting each one manually with no way to confirm they're a real, registered company.
- **No trust signal.** There's no easy way to know if a provider is a legitimate, DBD-registered business or just a good-looking landing page. Buyers carry all the risk.
- **Reaching out is a chore.** Getting quotes means contacting providers one by one, re-explaining the same brief every time, and waiting — often with no reply.
- **Providers rely on referrals and luck.** Good agencies and specialists depend on word-of-mouth and cold outreach. Great work goes undiscovered because there's no dedicated place for Thai businesses to find them.
- **The tools don't fit the market.** Global freelance and lead platforms aren't built for Thailand — they're English-only, ignore LINE (where Thai business actually happens), and lean on commissions and bidding wars.

The result: buyers overpay in time and risk, and capable providers stay invisible.

---

## The Solution

Profindle is a two-sided marketplace purpose-built for Thai B2B, serving both sides of the deal.

**For buyers**, it's the fastest way to find a trusted partner: search verified providers by service and province, or post one **broadcast request** — a short brief with budget and timeline — that fans out to every matching provider at once. Proposals come back within hours, not weeks.

**For providers**, it's a channel of warm, inbound leads: list your company, showcase a portfolio, earn a verified badge, and get notified on **LINE the instant** a buyer posts a request that matches your services.

Everything is **verified via DBD registration**, **bilingual in Thai and English**, and **free to join** — with **no commissions and no hidden fees, ever**. A single broadcast replaces dozens of cold emails; a verified badge replaces guesswork with confidence.

**How it works, in three steps:**
1. **Post a Request** — Describe what you need and your budget. Takes under 2 minutes.
2. **Providers Respond** — Matching providers get notified on LINE and respond within hours.
3. **Pick the Best Fit** — Compare, chat, and choose. No cold calls, no spam.

## Design Foundation

*This section is written to be pasted into an AI design tool to inform visual and brand updates. It captures the brand, audiences, screens, and emotional goal of the product.*

### Brand essence
Profindle is a **B2B marketplace for Thailand** connecting **buyers (businesses seeking services)** with **verified service providers**. The brand should feel **professional, trustworthy, modern, and Thai-market-aware** — polished enough for enterprise buyers, approachable enough for a two-person studio. Think "confident and efficient," not "flashy startup."

### Tone & personality
- **Professional & trustworthy** — this is business software handling real deals and money; credibility and verification are central.
- **Modern & clean** — generous whitespace, clear hierarchy, restrained UI. Content-first, low ornamentation.
- **Fast & effortless** — the product's core promise is speed and confidence; the design should feel frictionless (e.g., the hero is a single warm greeting, "Hello.", above a search box).
- **Thai-market-aware** — bilingual-first (Thai + English must both look intentional, never an afterthought), LINE as a first-class brand presence, local trust signals (DBD verification).

### Brand colors
| Role | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Primary (teal gradient)** | `#0F6F73` → `#1A9DA3` | Primary brand color, buttons, links, hero accents, gradients. Conveys trust, calm, professionalism. |
| **Accent (orange)** | `#F77F00` | Calls-to-action, highlights, "Early Bird" / premium moments, focal-point interactions, energy. Use sparingly for emphasis. |
| **Dark / ink** | `#171A21` | Text, headings, dark surfaces, footer. Near-black with a cool cast. |

- Use the **teal gradient** as the signature brand device (hero backgrounds, primary buttons, badges).
- Use **orange** as the single high-energy accent — CTAs, the Pro/premium badge, and "act now" moments — never as a background wash.
- Keep neutrals clean and cool-leaning to complement the teal; maintain strong contrast for readability in both Thai and Latin scripts.
- Support **light and dark themes**; teal and orange must remain legible and vibrant in both.

### Trust & status signals (visual system)
- **Verified badge** — a clear, reassuring mark (teal) on provider profiles and search cards.
- **Pro badge** — a premium mark (✦, orange/teal) for premium providers.
- **Plan states** — Free vs Premium should read clearly without feeling punitive toward free users.

### The two audiences
1. **Service Providers** — agencies, studios, law firms, accountants, event companies, and specialists across 400+ service categories. They want to be discovered, look credible, and receive leads on LINE. Design goal: make them feel *showcased and in-demand*.
2. **Buyers / Companies** — businesses looking for a trusted partner. They want speed, verification, and easy comparison. Design goal: make them feel *in control and confident*.

Many screens serve both; the landing page explicitly presents "For Buyers" and "For Providers" side by side.

### Key screens
1. **Landing page** — Warm minimal hero ("Hello." + "What service provider are you looking for?") over a prominent **search box** (service type + location). Below: a 3-step "How it works" (Post a Request → Providers Respond → Pick the Best Fit) and a two-column "Built for both sides of the deal" (For Buyers / For Providers). Primary CTAs: *Get Started Free*, *Browse Providers*.
2. **Provider search / discovery** — Search bar (service + province) with filters (Verified only, Premium only, province, budget). Result cards show logo, name, verified/pro badges, services, location. Emphasis on scannability and trust signals.
3. **Provider profile** — Company hero (logo, bilingual name, verified badge), description, services, team size, founded year, and a **portfolio grid** of projects (cover images, results, optional confidential clients). A LINE contact row for direct connection. This is the buyer's decision moment — make it credible and rich.
4. **Dashboard (provider home)** — "Welcome back" with a **Getting Started** checklist (complete profile → add portfolio → connect LINE → claim Early Bird), stat tiles (profile views, broadcasts, projects, verification status), quick actions, and recent activity. Feels like a helpful, momentum-building home base.
5. **Broadcast request (buyer flow)** — A guided multi-step wizard: **Service → Details & Budget → Budget & Delivery Date → Review**, ending in a success state ("Broadcast sent! Matching providers have been notified on LINE — {count} providers matched"). Should feel fast, clear, and reassuring (under 2 minutes).

Supporting screens: **My Company** (profile editing with AI auto-fill from website URL, DBD upload, logo, bilingual fields), **Portfolio** editor (image focal-point picker uses the orange dot), **Package/Pricing** (Free vs Premium with the Early Bird banner), **Settings** (LINE Connect flow, notifications), passwordless **auth** (email one-time code), and an **Admin panel**.

### Emotional goal
Every screen should deliver **confidence + speed** — the feeling of quickly and safely finding (or becoming) the *right* business partner. Buyers should feel *"I found someone I can trust, fast."* Providers should feel *"I look credible, and real leads are coming to me."* Reduce anxiety through verification and clarity; reward action through fast, LINE-connected momentum.
