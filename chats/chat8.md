## Assistant

You're right — promotions don't belong in Terms. Standard practice is:

- **Terms of Service** = the contract framework (account, content, liability, governing law)
- **Promotion terms** = separate, lives on the promo page or banner itself, has its own "valid until" / "subject to change" wording

Companies that do include billing in Terms only state the *structure* ("we offer paid plans, billing via X, cancel anytime") — never specific prices or promo mechanics, because those change too often. Since you're in trial mode and not charging yet, even that minimal structure can wait.

You're good to launch. ✅

Just keep a mental note: when you flip the Premium switch live and start collecting payment, add a short clause to Terms covering billing/refunds. That's the trigger, not now.
